Privacy Policy — DevelopmentALL Backup

Last updated: 2026-11-21

What this tool is

DevelopmentALL Backup is a personal backup tool run by one individual on his own computer. It copies his own files to his own Google Drive using rclone and restic.

What data it accesses

The tool accesses Google Drive only to upload, list, and restore the owner's own backup files. It does not read other people's files, and no other person uses this tool.

How data is used

Backup files are stored in the owner's own Google Drive account. The data is used only for backup and restore by the owner.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The tool has no other users.

Data retention and deletion

Backups remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from his Google Account settings, which stops all further access.
